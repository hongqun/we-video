## About We-Video

**Turn articles and knowledge into videos with voice and visuals, at a low cost.**

No expensive monthly video model subscriptions, advanced editing skills, or on-camera appearances required. **Just focus on your content.**

We-Video is an AI creation workspace for narrated explainer videos, with an indicative production cost of approximately **CNY 0.1 per minute**. Start with an article or reference material, then bring together scripts, voiceovers, subtitles, and visuals to share knowledge and ideas through video.

## Supported Scene Types

- **Animated character intro stage**: Introduce the topic with a character and an animated stage.
- **Text reveal**: Gradually reveal key text to pace the narration.
- **Progressive image reveal**: Unfold images alongside the explanation to highlight visual details.
- **Hand-drawn illustrations**: Upload line art and a colored image, then reveal outlines stroke by stroke and add color gradually.
- **Hand-drawn explainer**: Create illustrations and handwritten text with an Agent, revealing them stroke by stroke alongside the narration.
- **3D model explainer**: Use orbiting views, close-ups, and annotations to show an object's shape and structure.
- **Text highlights**: Highlight keywords in sequence with the narration to help viewers follow the main points.
- **General content cards**: Present titles, body text, and keywords clearly to convey knowledge and ideas.
- **Automatic diagrams**: Create diagrams with an Agent, progressively revealing processes, structures, and data alongside the narration.
- **Relationship diagrams**: Gradually reveal connections between concepts to make complex relationships easier to understand.
- **Animated character conclusion stage**: Wrap up the content and emphasize the conclusion with a character and an animated stage.

## Keep Your Focus on Content

Video creation often means switching between tools, from organizing source material to producing the final video. We-Video brings these steps into one workspace so creators can focus on what to say and how to say it.

- **Start with text**: Turn articles and reference material into narration that makes knowledge easier to understand.
- **Coordinate voice and visuals**: Build voiceovers, subtitles, and visual storytelling around your content.
- **Stay in control**: Review and adjust each stage, combining AI assistance with your own judgment.
- **Reduce production costs**: The indicative cost is approximately CNY 0.1 per minute. Actual costs depend on model choices, asset requirements, and the number of generation attempts.

## From Idea to Finished Video

**Provide an article or reference material → Refine the narration script → Generate voiceover → Arrange visuals → Preview the video**

Whether you are explaining a concept, interpreting an opinion, or exploring a topic, start with existing text and develop its voice and visuals step by step.
